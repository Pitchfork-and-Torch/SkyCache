Safe Water and Latrine Literacy (Historical Public-Health Summary)

IMPORTANT: Educational only. NOT engineering certification. NOT medical advice. Local authorities set standards for wells, tanks, and waste systems.

Open educational public-health materials long emphasized:

1. Keep drinking water sources separated from animal waste and latrines.
2. Cover stored drinking water; use clean cups; avoid dipping dirty hands into jars.
3. Boil water when boiling is feasible and fuel is available if contamination is suspected (historical advice; modern guidance may include other approved methods - follow local authority).
4. Latrines should drain away from wells and streams when terrain allows.
5. Wash hands after latrine use and before eating.
6. Protect children from playing near open waste.

Disaster settings increase contamination risk. Coordinate with trained WASH responders when present.

Matsuo Basho - selected haiku (public domain)
Romaji + English gloss

Furu ike ya / kawazu tobikomu / mizu no oto
  - Old pond - / a frog jumps in / water's sound

Natsu-gusa ya / tsuwamono-domo ga / yume no ato
  - Summer grasses - / all that remains / of warriors' dreams

Shizukasa ya / iwa ni shimi-iru / semi no koe
  - Stillness - / sinking into the rocks / cicada cry

Aki fukaki / tonari wa nani o / suru hito zo
  - Autumn deepens - / what does my neighbor / do for a living?

Note: Japanese originals are public domain (Basho d. 1694). This kit ships romaji so every node can render without CJK fonts. Operators may install full Japanese text locally.

Natural Selection (Educational Excerpt)

Darwin observed that individuals of a species vary, and that more individuals are born than can survive. Those variations that aid survival and reproduction tend to become more common in later generations - natural selection.

This idea organizes vast evidence from geology, breeding, and natural history. School kits should pair this excerpt with full public-domain editions when storage allows, and with local teacher guidance.

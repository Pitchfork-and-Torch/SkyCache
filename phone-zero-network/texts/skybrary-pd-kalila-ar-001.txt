Kalila and Dimna - The Crow and the Snake (traditional retelling)

A crow nested in a tree near a black snake that ate her young. In grief she asked a jackal for counsel. The jackal said: do not fight the snake with force; use craft. Scatter jewels from a nearby palace path so servants follow the trail into the snake's hole. When they dig for treasure, they will kill the snake.

The crow did so. The servants found the snake, slew it, and the crow's nest was safe again.

Moral (traditional): wisdom and patience outmatch raw strength.

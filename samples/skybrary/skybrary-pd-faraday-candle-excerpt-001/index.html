The Chemical History of a Candle (excerpt)

There is no better, there is no more open door by which you can enter into the study of natural philosophy than by considering the physical phenomena of a candle.

A candle is not a simple thing. Watch the flame: the dark centre, the bright region, the rising column of hot air, the soot that forms when the flame is disturbed. Air supports burning; without fresh air the flame dies.

Faraday taught that careful observation of ordinary things is the path into science. This excerpt is literacy for village STEM kits - full lectures are public domain.

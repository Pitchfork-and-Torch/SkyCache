Why Open Literacy Packs Matter (Operator Note)

A village node is not free commercial satellite broadband. It is a store-and-forward library: open books, health literacy, civics primary sources, and STEM primers that survive when the backhaul fails.

Dual access means the same works can be browsed online when a link exists, and served from USB, mesh, or SD when it does not. Every package should carry a license passport, SHA-256 integrity, and honest scope banners.

SkyCache / Skybrary expands only public-domain and clearly open-licensed materials. No commercial constellation decryption. No pirate mirrors. Fail closed on licenses.

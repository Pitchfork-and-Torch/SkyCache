Food Hygiene Literacy (Historical Household Summary)

IMPORTANT: Educational household literacy. NOT food-business certification.

Public-domain domestic science materials often stressed:

1. Wash hands before preparing food.
2. Keep raw meat separate from ready-to-eat foods when possible.
3. Cook thoroughly when fuel allows; reheat leftovers until steaming hot.
4. Cover food against flies.
5. Use clean utensils and clean water for washing produce when available.
6. Discard food that smells rotten or is moldy beyond cultural safe use.

Market and restaurant rules are set by local inspection authorities.

Seed Saving Literacy (Historical Educational Summary)

Farming communities have long selected seed from healthy plants, dried and stored seed away from moisture and pests, and shared varieties adapted to local seasons.

Basic literacy points:
1. Label seed with crop name, year, and field if known.
2. Dry thoroughly before sealed storage when climate allows.
3. Keep some seed for next season even after a good harvest.
4. Learn from neighbors which varieties handle local drought or rain.

Modern varieties, hybrid seed, and plant-health rules vary by country. This text is heritage literacy, not commercial seed law advice. Consult local extension services.

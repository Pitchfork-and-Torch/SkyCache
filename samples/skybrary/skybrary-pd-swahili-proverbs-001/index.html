Methali za Kiswahili / Swahili proverbs (traditional)

Haraka haraka haina baraka.
  - Haste has no blessing. (Slow careful work beats rush.)

Unity is strength: kidole kimoja hakivunji chawa.
  - One finger cannot kill a louse. (Cooperate.)

Asiyefunzwa na mamaye hufunzwa na ulimwengu.
  - Who is not taught by their mother is taught by the world.

Mwenye njaa hana miiko.
  - The hungry person has no taboos. (Need changes rules carefully.)

Bandu bandu humaliza gogo.
  - Chip by chip finishes the log. (Steady progress.)

Usisahau mizizi yako.
  - Do not forget your roots.

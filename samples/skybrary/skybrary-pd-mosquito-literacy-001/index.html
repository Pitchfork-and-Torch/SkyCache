Mosquito-Borne Illness Literacy (Historical Community Summary)

IMPORTANT: Educational only. Fever after mosquito exposure needs trained assessment. Not a substitute for national malaria/dengue programs or laboratory tests.

Historical tropical hygiene teaching often included:

1. Mosquitoes breed in standing water - empty or cover containers when practical.
2. Sleep under nets when nets are available and intact.
3. Seek care early for high fever, confusion, bleeding, or inability to drink.
4. Pregnant people and young children are often prioritized by health programs.
5. Insecticide and drug programs must follow trained campaign rules - do not improvise chemicals.

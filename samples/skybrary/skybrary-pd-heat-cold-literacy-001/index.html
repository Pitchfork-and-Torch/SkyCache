Heat and Cold Exposure Literacy (Historical Field Summary)

IMPORTANT: Educational only. Severe heat stroke and hypothermia are emergencies - seek trained care. Follow current wilderness and EMS guidance from certified sources.

Historical field manuals commonly taught community helpers to:

Heat:
- Move the person to shade; loosen tight clothing when safe.
- Offer sips of safe water if the person is awake and able to drink.
- Cool with wet cloths on skin when water is available; avoid overheating helpers.
- Do not force fluids into an unconscious person.

Cold:
- Move out of wind and wet ground when possible; remove wet outer layers if dry clothes exist.
- Insulate from the ground; share body warmth carefully without burns.
- Offer warm sweet fluids only if fully awake.
- Do not rub frostbitten areas with snow (historical warnings against folk harm).

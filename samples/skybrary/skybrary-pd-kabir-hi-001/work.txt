Kabir - selected dohas (public domain tradition)
ASCII transliteration for offline kits

Bura jo dekhan main chala, bura na milya koi;
Jo dil khoja apna, to mujhse bura na koi.
  - I set out to find the wicked; found none outside.
    Searching my own heart, none was worse than I.

Pothi padh padh jag mua, pandit bhaya na koi;
Dhai akshar prem ka, padhe so pandit hoi.
  - Reading stacks of books makes no sage;
    two-and-a-half letters of love make the wise.

Jaise til mein tel hai, jyon chakmak mein aag;
Tera sain tujh mein base, jaag sake to jaag.
  - Oil is in the sesame; fire is in the flint;
    the beloved dwells in you - awaken if you can.

Yoruba proverbs / Owe (traditional selection)

Ile la ti n ko eso r'ode.
  - Charity begins at home. (Character starts in the household.)

Bi a ba n gun'yan, bi a ba n se'be, ise ti a ba fi'wo le a ma dun ni.
  - Whatever work you put your hand to, do it well.

Oju l'oro wa.
  - The face carries the message. (Presence and respect matter.)

Agba ki i wa loja, k'ori omo titun wo.
  - When elders are in the market, a child's head is not left crooked.
    (Community protects the young.)

Igi kan ko le da igbo se.
  - One tree does not make a forest. (Need many people.)

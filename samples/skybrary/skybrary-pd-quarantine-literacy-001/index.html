Quarantine and Isolation Literacy (Historical Summary)

IMPORTANT: Educational only. Outbreak response is directed by national and WHO guidance. Do not invent isolation rules that contradict trained authorities.

Historical public-health writing often distinguished:

1. Isolation: keeping a sick person away from others when advised, with care for dignity and basic needs (food, water, clean bedding).
2. Quarantine: limiting movement of people who may have been exposed, when ordered by authority.
3. Caregiver protection: clean hands, separate utensils when recommended, and rest for helpers.
4. Honest information: rumors increase panic; listen to official channels.
5. Continuity of essential care: emergency childbirth, trauma, and chronic medicine needs still require trained workers even during outbreaks.

SkyCache operators in disaster mode should pair this literacy with local authority sheets under open licenses when available.

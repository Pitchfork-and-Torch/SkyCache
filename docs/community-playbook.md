# Community Deployment Playbook (one page)

**SkyCache Nexus** = small computers that **receive free public satellite information** (and/or USB education packs), share them on **local Wi-Fi**, and can form a **village mesh** so several buildings feel like one local library network.

It is **not** free Starlink or unlimited internet. It will not replace YouTube streaming. It **will** help with lessons, health flyers, weather pictures, emergency checklists, and village notes - with optional multi-node fabric (see `village-nexus-playbook.md`).

---

## Who does what

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Community lead | Chooses location, rules of use, content priorities |
| Local maintainer | Power, reboots, weekly check (trained) |
| Technical volunteer | First install, antenna aim, updates |
| Teachers / clinic staff | Request content; report broken links |

## 10 steps

1. **Agree goals** (school? clinic? both?).  
2. **Check rules** for WiFi hotspot and radio reception in your country.  
3. **Pick a safe place**: dry, lockable, solar possible, antenna sky view.  
4. **Build MVP kit** (see hardware BOM) - start **without** solar if needed.  
5. **Run simulation** on a laptop so teachers see the portal first.  
6. **Install on the hub** computer; load sample + local language packs.  
7. **Turn on WiFi** `SkyCache-Local`; post a paper sign with simple instructions.  
8. **Train two maintainers** (not just one): power lights, reboot, "is the portal up?"  
9. **Add real content**: Kiwix ZIM, health PDFs, MoE materials (with permission).  
10. **Schedule a monthly review**: storage free? battery healthy? what content is missing?

## Daily use (users)

1. Connect phone WiFi to **SkyCache-Local**.  
2. Open browser -> portal appears (or visit the address on the sign).  
3. Tap big icons: Emergency, Health, Education, Farm, Weather, Maps.  
4. Switch language at the top.  

## Weekly maintainer checklist

- [ ] Portal opens on a phone  
- [ ] Battery / solar charge OK  
- [ ] Enough free storage  
- [ ] Emergency and health content still visible  
- [ ] Dust/water check on enclosure  

## When something fails

1. Reboot the hub.  
2. Try another phone.  
3. Check power LED / battery.  
4. Call the technical volunteer with photo of lights.  

## Content priorities when space is full

**Keep:** Emergency -> Health -> Education -> Farm -> Weather -> Maps  
**Delete first:** old raw telemetry and unused large videos

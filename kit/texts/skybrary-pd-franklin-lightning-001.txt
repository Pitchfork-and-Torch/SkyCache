Lightning and Electricity (Historical Educational Summary)

Franklin's kite experiment and lightning-rod ideas taught that lightning is a form of electricity and that tall conductors can offer a safer path to ground for strikes when properly installed by competent people.

Community literacy points (historical framing only):
1. Storms can kill; seek sturdy shelter when lightning is near.
2. Tall isolated trees and open ridges are dangerous in storms.
3. Metal paths can carry current - modern buildings follow electrical codes.

IMPORTANT: This is historical science literacy, not electrical installation advice. Follow modern national codes and licensed electricians for any building work.
